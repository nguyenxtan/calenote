# Vietnamese lunar conversion

Status: local Conversation V2 implementation, not enabled on a deployed Worker.

The application uses a fixed UTC+07:00 Vietnamese civil-calendar convention
for lunar years 1900–2100, not a reconstruction of historical official timezones.
Scheduling instants remain a separate backend concern. No model, network, DB,
Chinese-calendar `Intl` oracle or astronomy calculation participates at runtime.

## Source and provenance

`tools/calendar/generate-lunar-vn.ts` generates month starts offline with exactly
Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 (MIT, Don Cross), a dev dependency. Its license is retained
by the package distribution; source: <https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy>.
It applies the published civil-day and leap-month rules from Hồ Ngọc Đức:
<https://www.xemamlich.uhm.vn/calrules.html> (updated 2008-08-18).
No calendar implementation from that article is copied into the application.

The generated artifact embeds the generator SHA-256 and month-data SHA-256.
Its version is `lunar-vn-utc7-ae2.1.19-v1`. Changes to ephemerides, rules or data
require a new conversion version and conformance review, not silent regeneration.
Generation calculates guard lunations outside the supported interval to determine
boundary month lengths. Only the supported lunar years are exposed by the adapter.

Regenerate offline:

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types tools/calendar/generate-lunar-vn.ts
```

## Independent acceptance data

`tools/calendar/reference-vectors.json` records independently published facts,
their sources and the access date. Unknown publication dates are explicitly null.
The 1900 and 2100 boundary vectors were read from the author's rendered calendar;
other vectors cover normal/leap months and Vietnamese/Chinese date differences
in 1984/1985/2007/2030/2053. Expected vectors are not generated by our converter.

Conformance tests additionally round-trip every supported day, reject impossible
normal/leap dates, and regenerate the complete month table with exact digest
equality. Round-trip tests prove internal consistency, not independent accuracy;
they supplement, not replace, the published reference vectors.

## Runtime behavior

Gregorian remains the default. Lunar mode needs explicit user intent for the
active request. Missing lunar year or ambiguous normal/leap month clarifies;
invalid or unsupported dates never fall back to Gregorian interpretation.
Persist the original lunar date, converted date and conversion version together
inside approved encrypted context/proposal storage. Nothing is scheduled before
explicit confirmation of the preview.
